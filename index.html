FFO per share growth

Change from the same quarter a year earlier, in percent. FFO (funds from operations) is the standard REIT earnings measure.

  • Essex and AvalonBay report Core FFO; Equity Residential reports Normalized FFO. Both remove one-off items, but not exactly the same ones.
  • Gaps are quarters where the filing printed no change as a dash ("—"), so there is no number to quote.
