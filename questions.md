# Research Questions: REIT Performance

**Topic:** Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs). How are they performing?
**As of:** October 2026

## 1. Overall performance
1. How have U.S. REITs (e.g., FTSE Nareit All Equity REITs, MSCI US REIT Index) performed year-to-date in 2026, and over the last 1, 3 and 5 years?
2. How does that compare with the S&P 500, bonds (U.S. Aggregate), and private real estate (e.g., NCREIF ODCE)?
3. How much of the total return comes from dividends and how much from price change?

## 2. Interest rates and macro drivers
4. How have changes in the 10-year Treasury yield and Federal Reserve policy in 2025–2026 affected REIT prices?
5. What is the current spread between REIT dividend yields and the 10-year Treasury, and how does it compare with the historical average?
6. How are inflation, economic growth and recession risk shaping the REIT outlook?

## 3. Sector breakdown
7. Which REIT sectors are leading and which are lagging in 2026 (data centers, industrial, residential/apartments, single-family rental, office, retail, healthcare/senior housing, self-storage, cell towers, net lease, hotels)?
8. What is driving the leaders (e.g., AI-driven data center demand, senior housing demographics)?
9. What is holding back the laggards (e.g., office vacancies, remote work, oversupply in apartments or industrial)?

## 4. Fundamentals
10. What are current trends in occupancy, rent growth, and same-store net operating income (NOI) by sector?
11. How are funds from operations (FFO/AFFO) growing, and are companies beating or missing guidance?
12. How healthy are REIT balance sheets: leverage, debt maturities, refinancing costs, and fixed- vs. floating-rate debt?

## 5. Valuation
13. Are REITs trading at a premium or discount to net asset value (NAV)?
14. How do price-to-FFO multiples compare with their own history and with the broader equity market?
15. Is there evidence of M&A, take-privates, or activist activity signaling that public REITs are undervalued?

## 6. Capital flows and sentiment
16. What are fund flows into REIT ETFs and mutual funds showing (e.g., VNQ, SCHH, XLRE)?
17. How are institutional investors and analysts positioning on REITs for 2027?

## 7. Global view (optional)
18. How are REITs performing outside the U.S. (Europe, UK, Japan, Singapore, Australia)?

## 8. Outlook and risks
19. What do major forecasters (Nareit, brokers, asset managers) expect for REIT returns over the next 12 months?
20. What are the key risks (rates staying higher for longer, commercial real estate credit stress, sector oversupply) and upside catalysts (rate cuts, a pickup in transactions)?

## Desired output
- A summary answer to "How are REITs performing?" with key figures and dates
- A table of sector returns (YTD and 1-year)
- A list of sources with publication dates; prefer data from Q3 2026 or later
